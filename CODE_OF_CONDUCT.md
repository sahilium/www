# Code of Conduct

This is a small personal website. There is exactly one maintainer. He is tired,
but grateful you're here.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to be a welcoming community for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, religion, or the OS they insist on using.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language (yes, even in commit messages).
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback, even when it comes with a
  snarky comment attached.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the ~4 daily visitors to
  sahil.im.
- Getting past your fear of `git rebase`.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments (directed at people OR at any
  framework that isn't the one you use).
- Publishing others' private information without permission, like your
  *actually* private key or the fact that you haven't pushed in 6 months.
- Harassment, public or private. Yes, even in the issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers — all one of them — are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
this code of conduct, and will take fair corrective action, which may include
a strongly-worded PR review, a heartfelt comment, or outright ignoring you.

Report violations by opening an issue (marked `violation`) or by glaring at
the maintainer until they notice. All complaints will be reviewed in the order
they arrive, which is to say whenever the maintainer has a free evening.
